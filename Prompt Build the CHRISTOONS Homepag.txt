Prompt: Build the CHRISTOONS Homepage
Role: You are a Lead Product Designer and Senior Frontend Engineer specializing in high-end, cinematic web experiences (React, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion).

Goal: Build a stunning, high-fidelity homepage for CHRISTOONS — a premium Christian animation platform for kids and teenagers. The UI must rival Disney+ and Netflix in polish but with a unique, warm, and inviting aesthetic.

1. Brand Identity & Theme
Name: "CHRISTOONS" (Single 'T').
Theme: "Chocolate & Glory". Instead of standard dark mode, use deep, rich browns and warm accents.
Color Palette:
Background: Deep Chocolate (#2B1B17 or #3E2723).
Accents: Gold (#FFD700) for divine elements/active states; Cream (#FFF8E1) for typography.
Vibe: Cozy, premium, velvety, "Heavenly Warmth".
2. Core Narrative Concept
The platform revolves around two modern families who "star" in the animated series:

The Freeman Family (Black): Parents + 3 Kids (Child to Teen).
The Sterling Family (White): Parents + 2 Kids (Child to Teen).
Storytelling Mechanism: The parents narrate Bible stories to the children, triggering a "Time Travel" transition where the modern kids enter biblical scenes.
3. UI Components & Layout
A. Navigation (Glassmorphic Chocolate Bar)
Position: Top or Left Sidebar (Sleek, semi-transparent).
Links: Home, Adventures (Core Series), Bible Stories (Time Travel), Songs/Worship, Parents Corner.
Style: Glassmorphism with a warm amber tint. Active links glow gold.
B. Hero Section: "The Cinematic Time Portal"
Visual: A massive, immersive top section (50% height).
Content: A composite visual of the children from the Freeman and Sterling families transitioning from a modern living room into a vibrant animated Bible scene (e.g., The Red Sea parting).
Copy: "Adventure Awaits in the Past!"
CTA Button: "travel Back" (Styled like an ancient scroll or golden time device).
C. Character Picker: "Who is Watching?"
Placement: Prominent row below the hero.
Function: Personalization.
Elements: Circular avatars representing the 5 children/teens from the two families.
Interaction: Hovering over an avatar adds a golden halo ring and slightly scales the image.
D. Content Rows (Netflix-Style)
Categories: "New Adventures with the Freemans", "Classic Bible Stories", "Worship Hits".
Cards: 16:9 thumbnails.
Effects:
Hover-Expand: Cards scale up (1.05x) smoothly.
Glow: Borders light up with a "Milk Chocolate" or Gold edge on hover.
4. Technical Requirements
Framework: React (Vite).
Styling: Tailwind CSS.
Animations: Framer Motion (Essential for the "premium" feel).
Smooth page transitions.
"Staggered" entrance animations for content rows.
Parallax effect on the Hero section.
Fonts: A friendly, rounded geometric sans-serif for headers (e.g., 'Outfit' or 'Fredoka'); clean sans-serif for body text (Inter).
Task: Generate the complete React code for this homepage, ensuring the "Chocolate & Glory" theme is implemented with exact hex codes and the specific family-focused narrative is reflected in the UI text and placeholders.
